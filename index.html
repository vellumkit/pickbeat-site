PickbeatA rhythm game in a red-rock quarry

Crystals grow out of the rock on the beat of the work song. Grip one, let go on its sweet spot and strike it free into the mine car. Five songs, one tempo, one shift.

Common questions

I bought something but it isn't unlocked, or I'm on a new device.
Open the STORE and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.
The beat feels early or late.
Bluetooth headphones and some speakers add a delay. In SETTINGS, under AUDIO TIMING, tap CALIBRATE and follow the beat.
How do I turn off ads?
Buy No Ads in the game's store. Rewarded ads are always optional.
How do I change my ad privacy choice?
Where the law requires it, SETTINGS shows AD PRIVACY, which reopens Google's consent form.
